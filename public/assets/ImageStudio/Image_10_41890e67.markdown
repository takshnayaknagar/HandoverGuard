## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a clinical-grade, modern visual language tailored for healthcare professionals operating in high-pressure shift environments. The brand personality is authoritative, calm, precise, and deeply trustworthy. The UI evokes a sense of absolute reliability and clarity, reducing cognitive load during critical patient handovers. 

Adopting a Corporate / Modern design movement, the visual framework prioritizes scannability, accessibility, and error prevention. High-contrast data grouping, generous touch targets, and a rigorous adherence to clinical workflow pacing ensure that practitioners can access vital patient data instantly without visual fatigue.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered for clinical legibility and instant status recognition. The primary deep medical blue establishes structural authority and calmness, while the accent teal guides user focus toward actionable metrics and interactive zones. Alert amber and coral serve as precise, high-visibility indicators for warnings and critical patient status changes. The background slate light provides a neutral, low-glare canvas that minimizes eye strain during prolonged night shifts.

## Typography

The typography system utilizes Inter for its exceptional legibility at small sizes and neutral, utilitarian character. Hierarchy is strictly enforced to ensure rapid scanning of patient vitals, medication schedules, and clinical notes. Headlines anchor major workflow stages, while body and label tokens maintain dense yet readable information architecture suitable for mobile viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout relies on a flexible single-column mobile grid system with consistent 16px outer margins and gutters. This fluid structure adapts gracefully across standard smartphones and clinical tablets, ensuring touch targets remain easily reachable within natural thumb zones. 

Component spacing adheres to a strict 4px/8px baseline grid. Padding within cards and list items is optimized to display dense medical data without feeling cramped. Minimum touch targets are locked at 48px to accommodate gloved interaction or hurried clinical inputs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established primarily through clean tonal layers and subtle ambient shadows rather than heavy borders. Surfaces sit on the light slate background with controlled elevation tiers:
- **Surface Level 0 (Base):** Canvas background (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards & Inputs):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surfaces with soft, diffuse shadows (`0px 2px 8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`) to lift interactive zones.
- **Surface Level 2 (Modals & Sticky Headers):** Elevated layers with pronounced shadow casting (`0px 8px 24px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`) to denote temporary floating states above the primary workflow.

## Shapes

The shape language employs a balanced roundedness model (Level 2), featuring 16px border radii (`rounded-2xl` equivalent) for primary cards and container blocks. This friendly yet professional curvature softens the dense clinical interface, preventing visual exhaustion while preserving a rigorous, structured hospital workflow aesthetic. Smaller interactive elements such as buttons and chips utilize 8px to 12px radii for consistent visual rhythm.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Primary actions utilize Deep Medical Blue (`#0F172A`) with high-contrast text, maintaining a strict minimum height of 48px. Secondary actions use subtle slate borders with teal text accents. Destructive actions deploy Coral (`#EF4444`).
- **Chips & Status Badges:** Pill-shaped or softly rounded tags used for quick status identification (e.g., Stable, Critical, Pending Review). Amber and Coral are reserved exclusively for warnings and urgent alerts.
- **Lists:** High-density patient record lists featuring clear separation lines, avatar indicators, and prominent primary metadata. Swiping actions must reveal quick-handover toggles.
- **Checkboxes & Radio Buttons:** Oversized, touch-friendly selection controls (minimum 24px tap areas embedded within 48px rows) designed for medication administration tracking and task completion checklists.
- **Input Fields:** Clean, bordered text inputs with floating labels, clear error states in Coral, and helper text zones. Designed to support rapid clinical note-taking and voice-to-text integration.
- **Cards:** 16px rounded patient summary containers featuring structured header zones for vital signs, collapsible body areas for handover notes, and color-coded left accent borders denoting acuity level.
- **Additional Clinical Components:** Bottom action sheets for quick shift sign-off, patient acuity progress bars, and floating sticky audio-recording bars for rapid voice memo handovers.
